About ReWear

What is ReWear?

ReWear is a sustainable second-hand clothing marketplace that allows people to buy, sell, rent, and donate pre-loved fashion. It helps extend the life of garments while making fashion more affordable and eco-friendly.

Why second-hand clothing?

Fast fashion contributes heavily to waste, harmful production practices, and overconsumption. Second-hand fashion gives clothes a longer life cycle while preserving their style and usefulness.

Environmental benefits

By reusing clothing, we reduce landfill waste, conserve natural resources, and reduce the demand for new textile production. Every item rescued helps support a more sustainable fashion system.

Affordable fashion

ReWear makes quality fashion more accessible by offering used clothing at budget-friendly prices. This allows individuals to refresh their wardrobe without paying full retail costs.

Reuse and recycling

Our platform encourages reuse, repair, and thoughtful consumption. When an item cannot be sold, it can be donated, reused by another person, or repurposed within the community.

Donation and sustainability

ReWear is built around the idea that fashion can be both stylish and responsible. Donating clothes creates positive impact by supporting communities, reducing waste, and encouraging mindful ownership.
